## GATE 0 — Fresh search evidence (before choosing work)

Run `~/.hermes/venvs/gsc/bin/python ~/.hermes/scripts/leadgen_gsc_report.py --days 28`
and read `~/antigravity/shared/leadgen-gsc-latest.md` before choosing a site or unit.
Visibility and indexing are separate measurements. Zero impressions or absence from
the performance export NEVER proves a page is not indexed. URL Inspection is sampled;
check its date and never generalise a handful of checked pages to the whole site.
Read report errors: failed queries are UNKNOWN, never zero traffic. If evidence for
the chosen site failed, resolve it or choose a site with valid evidence.

Prioritise real service pages and relevant research queries already earning impressions.
Keep all paused sites paused. Legal/contact pages do not drive SEO production.
Use the inspected URL's actual result:
- DISCOVERY: verify status, canonical, sitemap inclusion and existing internal links.
  Add useful contextual links only when missing; do not repeatedly link the same page.
- QUALITY_REVIEW: inspect relevance, duplication and usefulness before editing.
- INDEXED: use query intent, impressions, position and CTR to improve existing pages.
  Indexed with zero impressions does not establish that a page ranks or needs more copy.
- UNKNOWN or TECHNICAL_REVIEW: investigate the actual result before diagnosis.

Do not add pages merely to increase page count. New content needs demonstrated demand
and a distinct intent not already served. Preserve operator voice, working email lead
routing and the compact mobile layout. GBP is deferred until a site is rented.
No invented reviews, qualifications, addresses or job photos. Do not undo expandable
content just to increase visible word count. Respect the existing substance/rework gates.
Report the specific URL, dated evidence, change and intended outcome. Recheck rankings
and leads over time; do not claim an immediate ranking improvement from a deployment.
